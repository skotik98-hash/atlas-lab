## Execution guidance

- **SOP intake and coverage-tracking procedure** — Operations owns the T4 SOP library; this T3 playbook is the layer above it, responsible for tracking which repeated processes have a documented SOP and which do not. **Not Yet Exercised** — zero SOPs exist today.
- **Integration scorecard trigger conditions** — Per [`00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md` § Integration standards](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md#integration-standards-acquire--build--integrate), an integration scorecard opens the moment a new asset enters the Acquire/Build → Integrate transition. **Not Yet Exercised** — zero portfolio assets exist.
- **Incident response runbook pointer** — Any operational incident triggers root cause analysis within 5 business days, per [`00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md` § Incident response](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md#incident-response). **Not Yet Exercised** — zero incidents logged.
- **AI participation to specify** — Per [AI Participation Inside Departments, Operations row](03_ORGANIZATION.md#ai-participation-inside-departments): SOP execution assist, incident triage, and vendor ticket routing. None are live.

## Minimum executable step / checklist

- [ ] Any task performed three or more times per month is logged to an automation-candidate list owned by this playbook, per the [Automation by default](02_FOUNDING_PRINCIPLES.md#automation-by-default) threshold.
- [ ] The first time a repeated process is identified, open a T4 SOP draft for it before treating it as "handled."
- [ ] On any future incident, open an RCA within 5 business days per [Incident response](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md#incident-response).

This checklist is a **proposed procedure**; no automation-candidate list, SOP, or incident record exists yet — see [`05_CURRENT_STATE.md` § Current Operations](05_CURRENT_STATE.md#current-operations).
