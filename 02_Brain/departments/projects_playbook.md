## Mission

Deliver time-bound initiatives with clear accountability — transforming priorities into shipped outcomes, captured knowledge, and clean handoffs — per [`03_ORGANIZATION.md` § Department: Projects](03_ORGANIZATION.md#department-projects).

## Relationship to canonical Organization sections

This playbook does **not** restate Projects's scope, responsibilities, ownership table, inputs/outputs, KPIs, decision authority, or escalation rules — those are canonical in [`03_ORGANIZATION.md` § Department: Projects](03_ORGANIZATION.md#department-projects). This document covers execution mechanics only, per [One source of truth](02_FOUNDING_PRINCIPLES.md#one-source-of-truth).

A sequencing note: [Phase 1 exit criterion P1.2](04_ROADMAP.md#phase-1--operating-kernel) ("Project lifecycle used on ≥3 initiatives") depends on this playbook actually being *used*, not merely existing. ## Execution guidance

- **Intake → Triage → Brief → Plan → Execute → Review → Handoff checklist** — Operationalizes [`00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md` § Project Lifecycle](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md#project-lifecycle) stage by stage. **Not Yet Exercised** — zero projects have entered the intake stage, per [`05_CURRENT_STATE.md` § Current Projects](05_CURRENT_STATE.md#current-projects).
- **Brief template fields** — Scope and non-goals, success metrics, milestones and timeline, team and roles (with one named DRI), budget, risks and dependencies, and a handoff plan — per [`00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md` § Project Lifecycle § 3. Brief](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md#project-lifecycle). Having these fields ready means the first three real projects have a template to use immediately rather than inventing one under time pressure.
- **Retrospective-to-Knowledge handoff procedure** — Retrospectives transfer to Knowledge within 5 days of project close, per the KPI target in [`03_ORGANIZATION.md` § Department: Projects § KPIs](03_ORGANIZATION.md#department-projects).
- **AI participation to specify** — Per [AI Participation Inside Departments, Projects row](03_ORGANIZATION.md#ai-participation-inside-departments): status synthesis, risk flagging, and resource-conflict detection. None are live.

## Minimum executable step / checklist

- [ ] No project enters Execute without a named DRI and a one-sentence success metric recorded in the brief.
- [ ] Every project brief uses the field set above (scope, success metrics, milestones, team/DRI, budget, risks, handoff plan) before triage approval.
- [ ] Every closed project produces a retrospective transferred to Knowledge within 5 days, per the KPI target.

This checklist is a **proposed procedure**; no project brief, milestone review, or retrospective exists yet — see [`05_CURRENT_STATE.md` § Current Projects](05_CURRENT_STATE.md#current-projects).
