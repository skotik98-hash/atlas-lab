## Execution guidance

- **Pipeline intake and opportunity-brief template pointer** — Every opportunity, even informal, should produce a short opportunity brief before due diligence begins. **Not Yet Exercised** — zero opportunities on file, per [`05_CURRENT_STATE.md` § Current Assets](05_CURRENT_STATE.md#current-assets).
- **Due-diligence checklist skeleton** — A DD report structure, including the unconditional escalation rule that *any* new asset or venture escalates to Brain regardless of size, per [`03_ORGANIZATION.md` § Department: Assets § Escalation rules](03_ORGANIZATION.md#department-assets).
- **Exit-criteria check procedure** — Cross-referenced to [`00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md` § Exit criteria](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md#exit-criteria); this playbook is where the periodic check against those criteria should be scheduled once any asset exists.
- **AI participation to specify** — Per [AI Participation Inside Departments, Assets row](03_ORGANIZATION.md#ai-participation-inside-departments): DD document analysis, portfolio monitoring, and comparable-transaction benchmarking. None are live.

## Minimum executable step / checklist

- [ ] Every opportunity, even informal, gets a one-paragraph opportunity brief before any due-diligence work starts.
- [ ] Any new asset or venture, regardless of size or confidence, is escalated to Brain before proceeding to a term sheet, per [`03_ORGANIZATION.md`](03_ORGANIZATION.md#department-assets).
- [ ] Once any asset exists, schedule a recurring exit-criteria check per [`00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md` § Exit criteria](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md#exit-criteria).

This checklist is a **proposed procedure**; no opportunity brief, DD report, or exit check has ever been produced — see [`05_CURRENT_STATE.md` § Current Assets](05_CURRENT_STATE.md#current-assets).
