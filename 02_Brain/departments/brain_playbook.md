## Mission

Define Atlas's direction, maintain the decision and governance infrastructure, and ensure all departments execute against a coherent holding operating system — per [`03_ORGANIZATION.md` § Department: Brain](03_ORGANIZATION.md#department-brain).

## Relationship to canonical Organization sections

This playbook does **not** restate Brain's scope, responsibilities, ownership table, inputs/outputs, KPIs, decision authority, or escalation rules — those are canonically defined in [`03_ORGANIZATION.md` § Department: Brain](03_ORGANIZATION.md#department-brain) and must not be duplicated here per [One source of truth](02_FOUNDING_PRINCIPLES.md#one-source-of-truth). This document exists one layer below: **how** the Brain hat runs its recurring mechanics, not **who owns what**.

A note on scope, since it is genuinely unusual: Brain governs the other six departments, so a "Brain playbook" is slightly recursive — a playbook is normally how a domain *executes against* Brain's standards, not how Brain executes against itself. This stub deliberately stays narrow: it covers Brain's own recurring cadence (priority memo, escalation intake, quarterly review agenda), not a re-derivation of governance philosophy already owned by [`00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md`](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md) and [`02_FOUNDING_PRINCIPLES.md`](02_FOUNDING_PRINCIPLES.md).

## Execution guidance

- **Quarterly holding priority memo** — Brain synthesizes inputs from all six other hats (or, at Stage 0, the six other labeled dual-hat perspectives) into one priority stack. Cadence, input sources, and output format are proposed to follow the [Continuous improvement as a system](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md#continuous-improvement-as-a-system) quarterly rhythm — **Not Yet Exercised**; no priority memo has been produced to date (see [`05_CURRENT_STATE.md` § Current Brain Status](05_CURRENT_STATE.md#current-brain-status)).
- **Decision Gate certification** — For DL-3/DL-4 decisions, Brain is the escalation target of last resort and certifies [Gates 3–6](06_DECISIONS.md#decision-gates). This playbook is where that certification habit should live procedurally, distinct from the Decision Framework's definition of the gates themselves.
- **T1 document review cycle** — Brain tracks freshness of `00`–`07` against each document's `Review date` field and opens a Decision Record when a document lapses. This playbook (`brain_playbook.md` itself, once promoted past Draft) is subject to the same T3 review cadence.
- **AI participation to specify** — Per [AI Participation Inside Departments, Brain row](03_ORGANIZATION.md#ai-participation-inside-departments): strategy research drafts, decision-precedent retrieval, and principle-conflict flagging. No agent is registered for any of these yet — this is a proposed automation target, not a live capability.

## Minimum executable step / checklist

At each Brain-hat working session:

- [ ] Re-read [`05_CURRENT_STATE.md`](05_CURRENT_STATE.md) and [`06_DECISIONS.md`](06_DECISIONS.md) for drift against what this playbook and the T1 documents assume.
- [ ] Log any drift found as a Decision Record per the [Decision Record template](00_ATLAS_BRAIN.md#decision-record-template).
- [ ] Confirm no T1–T3 document is past its `Review date` without a scheduled action.

This checklist is a **proposed procedure**; it has not yet been run as a repeatable habit because no Brain-hat session has formally executed it end to end.
