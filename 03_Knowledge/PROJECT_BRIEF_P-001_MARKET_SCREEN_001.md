## 1. Why this Brief exists

Real Project Lifecycle work already happened inside P-001 — five candidate opportunities were identified, researched, scored, and triaged to a decision (Primary / Secondary / Deferred) — but it happened using the project's own informal structure (`PROJECT_BRIEF.md` §6, `OPPORTUNITY_REGISTER.md`, `ATLAS_MARKET_SCREEN_001.md`), not the Brain's canonical Project Brief fields. [Phase 1 exit criterion P1.2](../02_Brain/04_ROADMAP.md#phase-1--operating-kernel) requires evidence of "Project lifecycle used on ≥3 initiatives — Briefs + retrospectives in Knowledge." This document supplies the missing Brief-side artifact, and its companion [`RETROSPECTIVE_P-001_MARKET_SCREEN_001.md`](RETROSPECTIVE_P-001_MARKET_SCREEN_001.md) supplies the retrospective, both filed in Knowledge per the Projects department's [Retrospective-to-Knowledge handoff procedure](../02_Brain/departments/projects_playbook.md).

Per [One source of truth](../02_Brain/02_FOUNDING_PRINCIPLES.md#one-source-of-truth), this Brief **links to** the underlying P-001 documents rather than restating their content. Treat this file as the governance wrapper, not a replacement source.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:**

- Identifying candidate low-capital digital opportunities across the categories defined in `PROJECT_BRIEF.md` §4 (digital media, AI/automation, crypto, digital products, services).
- Scoring and ranking those opportunities using a consistent evaluation framework (`ATLAS_MARKET_SCREEN_001.md` §2, §7).
- Triaging each opportunity to an explicit decision: Primary candidate, Secondary candidate, or Deferred.
- Producing an experiment-ready brief for the primary candidate (`OP-022_AI_AUTOMATION_EXPERIMENT.md`).
- Capturing customer-discovery and customer-acquisition feasibility groundwork ahead of any paid pilot (`CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY_ENGINE.md`, `CUSTOMER_ACQUISITION_ENGINE.md`, `OPPORTUNITY_REGISTER.md` § Customer Acquisition Feasibility).

**Out of scope (for this sub-initiative):**

- Committing capital to any opportunity. Capital committed to date across all opportunities is **$0**.
- Running a paid pilot or customer-discovery outreach campaign. `OP-022` Experiment 001 status is explicitly "Not started."
- Selecting a final single venture. Atlas's stated philosophy (`OPPORTUNITY_REGISTER.md` §1) is a portfolio of experiments, not one bet.

## 9. Handoff plan

When this sub-initiative closes (i.e., when Initiative A either reaches a paid pilot outcome or is killed per `OP-022` §17 Decision Rules), the handoff is:

| Outcome type | Handoff to |
|---|---|
| Scale / Iterate (demand evidence found) | Remains with the Projects/Assets hats; may generate a new Decision Record and a follow-on Brief for the Build stage |
| Hold (insufficient evidence, low opportunity cost) | Stays in the Opportunity Register at "Research" status; revisited at next quarterly review |
| Kill (demand disproven or economics poor) | Retrospective updated with the kill rationale; capital and attention reallocated to Initiatives B/C/D/E per the ranking in `ATLAS_MARKET_SCREEN_001.md` §2 |

This Brief itself does not close until Initiative A resolves to one of the above.
